The project arc

Jun 7–9 · ~46 commits

Foundation & the element set

Phases 0–3 plus the full toolbox: numbers (exact rationals, manual ops), text, boxes, nests & birds, the scale, robots, the three tools (Dusty / Wand / Pumpy), the bomb, and live sensors — each checked against the manual and the original C++.

Jun 9–14 · the city, ported faithfully

Fly · land · walk · room

A pure fly/land/walk state machine, then a faithful port of the original's camera and coordinate system (screen.cpp/prgrmmr.cpp): green-grid flyover with a big helicopter, landing, 8-way street walking with camera-follow, entering houses, and the perspective room you stand in before sitting at the floor — matched to reference frames.

Jun 15–16 · ~43 commits

Room, notebook & animations

The grey-Lego Tooly toolbox + tan floor, the two-page spiral notebook spread, box grab-vs-extract, and real animations: the nest egg cracking and the bird flying up, bird flight on delivery, Bammer's hammer swing. Dusty and bomb behavior audited to the source.

Jun 17–19 · ~42 commits

Robot training & teams

Train by example in a full-screen thought bubble where you control the robot: take-out / put-in / wand-copy / Dusty-erase, with the result landing on Bammer's strike. Trained robots replay step-by-step, iterate as guarded loops, wait on incomplete boxes, form teams, and recurse through modules.

Jun 20 · 22 commits

Assets & held tools

Precise sprite transparency (keying the reserved palette index — no more moth-eaten holes), directional hair/hat head overlays for the avatar, and the held-tool morph (Lego ↔ clay) for Pumpy/Dusty/wand.

Jun 21–22 · this session

Polish, autonomy & fidelity capture

Fixed a Pointer-Lock cursor-trap, cleaned the held tools & toolbox, built a headless __ttDev debug-driver, added edge-scroll (floor + city), made robots keep working across views, drove the original ToonTalk via computer-use to capture reference, reworked the standing-room view to floor scale, and wrote a hand-off note. (11 commits.)
